Potionwise Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Potionwise game for Android (package com.cyberfreezedev.potionwise) uses, why, who receives it and what choices you have. Potionwise is published by CyberFreezeDev LLP ("CyberFreezeDev", "we", "us").

In short
  • No account, no sign-in, no purchases, and no servers of our own. We never receive your game data.
  • Your level, stars, lives, Daily Brew streak and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • There are no forced video ads. Videos play only when you choose to watch one for an extra life, an extra flask or more undos. After a win (from level 10) the results screen may show one small text-and-image ad card. All ads come from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure them, as described below.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices (where your region requires it) and in your phone's ad settings.

1. Who we are

CyberFreezeDev LLP (LLPIN ACK-4469), Ayapakkam, Chennai 600077, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact for anything in this policy: hello@cyberfreezedev.com. For the purposes of data protection law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR), we are responsible for the app. Google is independently responsible for the data its ads service collects, under its own policies.

2. Data that stays on your device

To remember your progress, Potionwise saves a small file on your phone with your current level, stars, lives and their refill timer, Daily Brew streak and streak freezes, a few gameplay counters (such as how often a level was retried, used to offer hints), and settings (sound, music, vibration, motion, text size, larger potion symbols, pour hints, graphics quality). This file never leaves your phone: we do not upload it, back it up to our servers or share it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. The game works fully offline.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

3. Data collected for ads (Google AdMob)

When you tap Watch video (or Watch for more undos), the game asks Google AdMob for an ad. From level 10, the results screen after a win may also show one small text-and-image ad card. To be ready, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may load these ads in advance while the game is running. The SDK sends the following to Google:

DataWhat it isWhy
Device or other IDs Your device's advertising ID and the app set ID Showing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention; personalised ads only where allowed and, where required, with your consent
Approximate location Worked out from your IP address (not GPS) Showing ads relevant to your region, legal compliance, fraud prevention
App interactions That the app was opened, ad views, taps on ads, video completion Ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics and crash logs Device model, Android version, app version, SDK performance and crash data Making ads work reliably, analytics

This matches what Google publishes for the Mobile Ads SDK (Google's data disclosure) and our Data safety section on Google Play. We do not receive this data ourselves, apart from aggregated, non-identifying reports from AdMob (for example how many videos were shown).

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers on your device and may combine this data with other data they hold, under their own policies. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your ad choices and consent

  • Watching is always optional. No ad plays unless you tap Watch video. Every level can be played without ads.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: the first time you start the game, Google's consent form (a Google-certified consent tool) asks whether ads may use your data and be personalised. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, as easily as you gave it, in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices.
  • United States: where a state privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, the same Ad choices button lets you opt out.
  • On any Android phone: you can reset your advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID) or delete it (Android 12 and later: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID). After deleting it, apps see only zeros instead of the ID, and ads are not personalised with it. You can also manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.
  • If you decline consent or opt out, the game still works and you may still see non-personalised reward videos if you choose to watch one.

5. Why we may use data (legal bases)

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, the game relies on your consent for storing and reading identifiers on your device and for personalised ads, collected through Google's consent form. Non-personalised ad delivery, ad measurement and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests (keeping the game free and protecting it from abuse). Where India's DPDP Act applies, data is processed for the specified purpose of showing the optional reward videos you request, on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw as described above.

6. Who receives data

Only Google (Google LLC and its affiliates) through the AdMob service, and the advertising partners Google works with to fill an ad, as described in section 3. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we hold almost none.

7. International transfers

Google processes ad data on servers in many countries, including the United States. Google states that it relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (see Google's data transfer frameworks).

8. How long data is kept

  • Game progress on your phone: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Ad data held by Google: for the periods set by Google (see How Google retains data).
  • Emails you send us: for as long as needed to answer you, then deleted, normally within 12 months.

9. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK travels over encrypted (TLS) connections. Because we run no servers for the game and receive no personal data from it, there is no database of player data that could leak from us. Your progress file is protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. Children

Potionwise is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that sent data to Google, write to us and we will help, including by asking Google to delete it where possible. Younger players in some countries may need a parent's consent to agree to ad personalisation; if in doubt, choose not to consent in the Ad choices form.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: know what data is processed about you and get a copy; have it corrected or erased; withdraw consent; object to or restrict processing; data portability; opt out of targeted advertising; nominate someone to exercise your rights (India); and complain to a regulator. Because we do not hold your game data or ad data, the fastest way to exercise most of these rights is through the in-app Ad choices button, your phone's ad settings, or Google directly (Google data & privacy). You can also write to us and we will help.

You may complain to your local data protection authority: in India, the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU/EEA, your national supervisory authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

12. Questions, requests and complaints

Email hello@cyberfreezedev.com with the subject "Potionwise privacy". This address is our contact for privacy questions and grievances. We acknowledge requests within 7 days and reply in full within 30 days, and always within the time the applicable law requires (for India, no later than 90 days).

13. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to use data differently, we will update this page, change the date at the top, and where the law requires it, ask for your consent again in the app before the change applies.
